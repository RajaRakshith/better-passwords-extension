Private by design.

  • Your passwords stay with AppleEach login comes from the password helper built into macOS, over Apple's own encrypted pairing, straight into the page.
  • No backend or trackingWe run no backend. Hide My Email connects to Apple only when you turn it on.
  • Open sourceThe extension and the companion app are public on GitHub under the MIT license, so anyone can check what they do.
  • The risks, written downA threat model lists what it defends against and what it doesn't.
