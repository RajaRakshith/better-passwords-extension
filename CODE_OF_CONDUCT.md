# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in project spaces or in public when representing the project.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/fanckush/better-passwords-extension/security/advisories/new) (it is private), marked "Code of Conduct". Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may ban contributors, as described in the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
